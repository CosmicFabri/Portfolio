Smiley Vision is a B2B optical distributor that provides optical and vision-care products, including laboratory equipment, optical frames, and lenses. The platform is exclusively available to optical professionals, as these products are not intended for purchase by the general public.
::

## Why build it

Smiley Vision is a medium-sized company, currently working in Mexico only, and with plans of expanding to other countries. The products it offers are specialized to the niche, so, one of the goals of this software is to correctly represent all of their particularities that no other off-the-shelf software does, including their specific features and attributes, as well as specialized attention to the optical professionals —our clients—, among other things.

::article-paragraph
This software is designed to cover the specific needs of our optical professionals — they want to have the best technology and quality in the vision-care products they acquire for serving their clients and patients. Smiley Vision aims to display such products and to give a custom, professional experience for every professional, ultimately taking care of the final users’ health.
::

## What I learned

- Knowing the requirements well enough is already half of the work
- A well-designed system and API makes the rest of the work easier
- Frontend architecture and development can be more challenging than it appears
