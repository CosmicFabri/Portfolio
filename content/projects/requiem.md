Requiem is a 3D game made with Godot that simulates the journey of Dante through heaven, purgatory and hell — each level takes you into a deeper step of the journey, and the enemies become scarier and harder to beat.
::

## Inspiration and mechanics

Dante, in his work The Divine Comedy, goes through 9 circles representing heaven, purgatory and hell. The deeper Dante goes, the worse the sins he sees. This game is inspired by this classical work and tries to give that same feeling to the player.

::article-paragraph
In each level, the player needs to kill the monsters (representing the sins) with a gun. The monsters will appear from spawners, and each level will bring more and heavier ones. Additionally, the game includes two bosses, the last of which represents the sin of treachery. The player needs to be careful not to be pushed by the enemies when colliding.
::
