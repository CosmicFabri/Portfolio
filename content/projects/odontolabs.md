OdontoLabs is a school management system designed specifically for the College of Dentistry of the Autonomous University of Campeche (UAC) — it provides school management and clinical treatments operations.
::

## The need for the software

The College of Dentistry has been facing two important problems regarding grading the students’ assignments through clinical treatments:

- There’s a lot of paperwork involved in doing these assignments; this means that the college stacks a lot of paper waste, which is harmful to the environment.
- It is difficult to preserve the data integrity of the patients due to some students tinkering with the clinical records (cheating with them, creating duplicate records).

::article-paragraph
Due to these problems, a software system was required so the college always preserves data integrity and reduces the use of paper. OdontoLabs cleanly solves these problems, and it brings a better, quicker way for students and faculty to manage assignments and school-related operations.
::

## Things I’ve learned

- A robust, maintainable system isn’t something that gets done in a few weeks — I had to go through hard times understanding the architecture of multi-step forms, as well as the logic of application-wide state management.
- It is actually effortless to navigate through (and add software components to) a well-structured application, especially a front-end one: my team and I agreed on a certain structure at the very beginning of the project, and now it’s easy to look for a component or form and to add new software modules.
- Constant meeting with the client and a clear understanding of the requirements are crucial for a seamless software development experience. I’m thankful to the College of Dentistry personnel for always trying to be on the same page with us software engineers; it made things way easier, and our tasks very clear.
